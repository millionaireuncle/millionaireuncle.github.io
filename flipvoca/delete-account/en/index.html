Flip Voca Account Deletion

How to delete your Flip Voca account and study records.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Flip Voca and tap Settings at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap [Delete] to confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Request without the app

If you have removed the app or cannot use it, email "Account deletion request" to millionaireuncle720@gmail.com from the Google account email you used to log in. We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Account information: email address, name, profile picture URL, account identifier
  • Study records: review schedules and ratings for each card, completed stages

Everything is deleted immediately and nothing is kept. Error reports, which are not linked to your account, are deleted automatically after up to 90 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
