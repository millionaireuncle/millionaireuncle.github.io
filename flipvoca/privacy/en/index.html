Flip Voca Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

millionaireuncle ("we"), owned by JongCheol Yeom, operates the Flip Voca app (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App processes, why, and when it is deleted.

1. Information we process

You can use the App without logging in. In that case your study records are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

  • When you log in: your Google account email address, name, profile picture URL (as provided by Google at login), account identifier, and login times.
  • Study records when logged in: review schedules and ratings for the word cards you studied, and the stages you completed.
  • Advertising: advertising identifier (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking), device and network information, and IP address. Google AdMob uses these to show ads.
  • Error information: when the App crashes or hits an error, the error details, device model, and OS and app versions. Your name, email, and IP address are not sent with it.

2. Why we process it

  • To back up your study records, sync them across devices, and manage your account
  • To show ads that keep the App free
  • To find the causes of errors and make the App more stable

Review reminders are scheduled on your device; no personal information is sent to a server for them.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Account information and study records: kept until you delete your account, then deleted from our server and your device without delay.
  • Error information: kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Advertising information: kept and deleted by Google under Google's policies.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correction, and deletion: go to Settings → Account → Delete account in the App to delete your account and study records right away. If you cannot use the App, email us (see Delete Account).
  • Opting out of personalized ads: on iOS, use the tracking prompt shown at first launch or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Users in the EEA and UK: the App asks for ad consent at first launch, and you can change it anytime in Settings → Account → Ad privacy settings.

7. Security

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Study records on our server can only be read and written by your own account.
  • Only we have administrative access to the server.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we collected such information without parental consent, we will delete it without delay.
